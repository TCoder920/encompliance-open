# How to Connect Your Google Calendar

Follow these simple steps to sync your Google Calendar with EnCompliance:

## Step 1: Click "Connect Google Calendar"

1. **Start the Connection**
   - Click the "Connect Google Calendar" button in EnCompliance
   - A new window will open with Google's authorization page

## Step 2: Sign In to Google

1. **Choose Your Account**
   - Select the Google account that has the calendar you want to sync
   - If you're not signed in, enter your Google credentials

2. **Review Permissions**
   - Google will show you what EnCompliance wants to access
   - EnCompliance only requests calendar access, nothing else
   - Click "Allow" to grant permission

## Step 3: Complete the Connection

1. **Authorization Complete**
   - The popup window will close automatically
   - You'll see your Google Calendar appear in the "Connected Calendars" section
   - Your calendar is now ready to sync!

## Step 4: Choose What to Sync

- **Import Only**: Bring your Google Calendar events into EnCompliance
- **Two-Way Sync**: Keep both calendars in sync (recommended)

## Troubleshooting

### "Authorization Error" or "Missing client_id"
- This means the Google Calendar integration isn't fully configured yet
- Contact your administrator to complete the Google Cloud setup
- This is a one-time setup that needs to be done by the EnCompliance team

### "Access Denied" Error
- Make sure you clicked "Allow" on the Google authorization page
- Try the connection process again
- Check that you're using the correct Google account

### Events Not Syncing
- Make sure you selected the correct calendar to sync
- Check that events aren't set to "Private" in your Google Calendar
- Try disconnecting and reconnecting your calendar

### Popup Window Blocked
- Your browser might be blocking popups
- Allow popups for the EnCompliance website
- Try again after enabling popups

## Security Notes

- Google OAuth is the industry standard for secure calendar access
- EnCompliance only accesses your calendar data, not emails or other Google services
- You can revoke access anytime from your Google Account settings
- Your calendar data is encrypted and stored securely

## Managing Your Connection

Once connected, you can:
- **Import**: Bring Google Calendar events into EnCompliance
- **Sync**: Keep both calendars updated with changes
- **Disconnect**: Remove the connection anytime

## Need Help?

If you're still having trouble, contact our support team with:
- The error message you're seeing
- Your Google account email address
- What step you're stuck on
