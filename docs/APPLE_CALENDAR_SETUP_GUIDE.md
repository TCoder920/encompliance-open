# How to Connect Your Apple Calendar

Follow these simple steps to sync your Apple Calendar with EnCompliance:

## Step 1: Generate an App-Specific Password

1. **Go to Apple ID Settings**
   - Visit [appleid.apple.com](https://appleid.apple.com)
   - Sign in with your Apple ID

2. **Navigate to Security Section**
   - Click on "Sign-In and Security"
   - Find the "App-Specific Passwords" section

3. **Create New Password**
   - Click "Generate Password" or the "+" button
   - Enter a label like "EnCompliance Calendar"
   - Click "Create"

4. **Save the Password**
   - Copy the generated password (it looks like: `abcd-efgh-ijkl-mnop`)
   - **Important**: Save this password somewhere safe - you won't be able to see it again!

## Step 2: Connect in EnCompliance

1. **Open Calendar Settings**
   - Go to your EnCompliance calendar
   - Click "Calendar Settings"

2. **Connect Apple Calendar**
   - Click "Connect Apple Calendar"
   - Enter your Apple ID email address
   - Paste the app-specific password you generated in Step 1
   - Click "Connect"

## Step 3: Choose What to Sync

- **Import Only**: Bring your Apple Calendar events into EnCompliance
- **Two-Way Sync**: Keep both calendars in sync (recommended)

## Troubleshooting

### "Invalid Credentials" Error
- Double-check your Apple ID email address
- Make sure you're using the app-specific password, not your regular Apple ID password
- Try generating a new app-specific password

### "Connection Failed" Error
- Check your internet connection
- Ensure you have iCloud Calendar enabled on your Apple device
- Try again in a few minutes

### Events Not Syncing
- Make sure you selected the correct calendar to sync
- Check that events aren't set to "Private" in your Apple Calendar
- Try disconnecting and reconnecting your calendar

## Security Notes

- Your app-specific password is stored securely and encrypted
- You can revoke the app-specific password anytime from your Apple ID settings
- EnCompliance only accesses calendar data, not other iCloud information
